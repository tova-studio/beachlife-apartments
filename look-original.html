Good to know

Questions guests often ask

How many people can stay in an apartment?

Our 16 self-contained units have one to three bedrooms and sleep 2 to 8 people. Seven have sea views.

What's included in each apartment?

A full kitchen, a large private balcony or deck and a 3.6kW heat pump/air-conditioner. You also get a 32" LCD HD TV with 50+ digital channels, free Wi-Fi and free local calls.

Is there parking at BeachLife Apartments?

Yes, there's secure parking on site.

How far is it to the beach in Christchurch?

About 100m. We're on Marine Parade in North Beach, and QEII Park and New Brighton Pier are about 1.5km away.

Is it suitable for a work trip or relocation?

Yes. The apartments suit people on holiday, on business or on transfer. Burwood Hospital, The Palms Shopping Centre and a Countdown supermarket are all close by.

How do I make a reservation?

Call 0800 72 2008 or 03 382 5688 from within NZ, or +64 3 382 5688 from overseas. You can also email info@beachlife.kiwi.
